# Ovia Inc — Artificial Intelligence

We created this repository as a public starting point for our broader artificial intelligence work — the learning, exploration, and reference side of the AI practice behind our production services.

Artificial intelligence covers a wide field: models, agents, data, evaluation, and the context that ties them together. We keep this repo open as the broad, foundational companion to our focused production work, so the thinking and groundwork behind that work stay visible in plain language.

**Our main site:** https://oviainc.github.io/ai-services/

## How this repository differs from ai-services

- **This repository (artificial-intelligence)** — the broad foundation: exploration, learning material, and reference work across artificial intelligence as a field. Early and general by design.
- **[ai-services](https://github.com/Oviainc/ai-services)** — the production side: our flagship site and the services we deliver for business, including AI agents, machine learning in production, and AI products.

Keeping the two separate keeps the promise of each one clear: exploration here, delivery there.

## How this fits our work

- **AI agents** — capable agents rest on a clear understanding of the wider field: what models can and cannot do, where they fail, and how to evaluate them before they reach real work.
- **Context engineering** — deciding what information a model receives, in what shape, and at what moment is applied artificial intelligence at its most practical, and the broader study gathered here informs those choices.
- **Business intelligence and data** — analysis, reporting, and forecasting are long-standing applications of artificial intelligence, and the foundations explored here support our data work.
- **Virtual production** — image, video, and language models continue to reshape production workflows; tracking that wider field here keeps our production practice grounded in what the technology actually does.
